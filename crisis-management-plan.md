## **1. PURPOSE**

The purpose of this Crisis Management Plan is to establish a framework
for handling crises that may disrupt the operations of **[Insert
Company Name]**, ensuring a timely and coordinated response to
minimize damage and ensure business continuity.

## **2. SCOPE**

This plan applies to all employees, contractors, and partners of
**[Insert Company Name]** and covers all types of crises,
including natural disasters, cybersecurity incidents, reputational
damage, and workplace accidents.

## **3. OBJECTIVES**

- To ensure the safety of employees, clients, and stakeholders.

- To provide a clear chain of command for decision-making during a
crisis.

- To minimize operational disruptions.

- To protect the company's assets and reputation.

- To restore normal operations as quickly as possible.
