### ARTICLE V: TRANSFER OF SHARES

**5.1 Restrictions on Transfer:**

No Shareholder shall sell, transfer, or otherwise dispose of any Shares
in the Company except in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

**5.2 Right of First Refusal:**

If a Shareholder wishes to sell or transfer their Shares, the other
Shareholders shall have a right of first refusal to purchase those
Shares on the same terms.

**5.3 Procedure for Transfer:**

A Shareholder who wishes to transfer their Shares shall provide written
notice to the Company and the other Shareholders. The notice must
include the number of Shares to be transferred and the price.

---

### ARTICLE VI: DIVIDENDS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**6.1 Dividends:**

The Company may, at the discretion of the Board of Directors, declare
dividends to be paid to the Shareholders based on their ownership
percentages, provided that the Company has sufficient profits.

**6.2 Distribution of Profits:**

Profits shall be distributed among the Shareholders in proportion to
their ownership percentages unless otherwise agreed in writing by the
Shareholders.

---

### ARTICLE VII: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**7.1 Mediation:**

In the event of a dispute arising under this Agreement, the Shareholders
agree to first attempt to resolve the dispute through mediation.

**7.2 Arbitration:**

If mediation is unsuccessful, the dispute shall be submitted to binding
arbitration in accordance with the rules of the **[Insert Arbitration
Organization]**.

---

### ARTICLE VIII: CONFIDENTIALITY

Each Shareholder agrees not to disclose any confidential information
related to the business, finances, or operations of the Company to any
third party without the express written consent of the Company or as
required by law.

---

### ARTICLE IX: TERMINATION

This Agreement shall terminate upon the occurrence of any of the
following events:

- The unanimous agreement of all Shareholders.

- The dissolution or liquidation of the Company.

- A sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets.
