### **1. Purpose**

The purpose of this Insider Trading Policy (the "Policy") is to promote
compliance with securities laws and regulations prohibiting insider
trading by employees, officers, directors, and other stakeholders of
**[Company Name]** ("the Company").

---

### **2. Scope**

This Policy applies to all employees, officers, directors, consultants,
and any other individuals who have access to Material Non-Public
Information ("MNPI") regarding **[Company Name]** or any other
companies with which **[Company Name]** does business. This also
includes members of their immediate family, household members, and any
entities over which such persons exercise control.

---

### **3. Definition of Insider Trading**

Insider trading refers to buying, selling, or otherwise trading in
securities (including stocks, bonds, and options) while in possession of
material non-public information. It is illegal and unethical for any
individual to use MNPI for personal financial gain or to provide such
information to others for personal financial gain.

---

### **4. Material Non-Public Information (MNPI)**

Material Non-Public Information is information that:

- Has not been disclosed to the general public;

- Is specific to **[Company Name]** or another company; and

- Could influence an investor's decision to buy, sell, or hold
securities if known.

Examples include:

- Earnings results or financial forecasts;

- Mergers, acquisitions, or joint ventures;

- Changes in senior management or corporate strategy;

- Significant contracts or new product launches;

- Major litigation or regulatory issues.

---

### **5. Policy on Trading in Company Securities**

No individual covered by this Policy may:

- Buy, sell, or otherwise trade in **[Company Name]** securities
while in possession of MNPI;

- Engage in transactions that might suggest they are trading on inside
information (e.g., short selling, trading in derivatives related to
Company securities);

- Pass MNPI on to others (this includes family members or friends).

**[Company Name]** securities include:

- Common stock, preferred stock, bonds, options, or any other financial
instruments issued by **[Company Name]**.

---

### **7. Blackout Periods**

Individuals with regular access to MNPI may only trade in **[Company
Name]** securities during approved "window" periods and are
prohibited from trading during blackout periods, which include:

- The period beginning __ days prior to the end of each fiscal
quarter and ending after the second full business day following the
public release of earnings information.

- Special blackout periods declared by the Company due to other
material events or developments.

---

### **8. Consequences of Violations**

Violation of insider trading laws and this Policy may result in severe
consequences, including:

- Disciplinary actions by **[Company Name]**, including
termination of employment or removal from the board;

- Civil and criminal penalties under federal and state laws, including
fines and imprisonment;

- Reputational harm to both the individual and **[Company
Name]**.
