**1. Purpose**

This memo serves to inform all employees of the upcoming blackout period
regarding the trading of [Company Name] stock and accessing non-public
information.

**2. Blackout Period Dates**

The blackout period will commence on **[Start Date]** and will end
on **[End Date]**.

**3. Reasons for Blackout Period**

During this blackout period, all employees are prohibited from trading
[Company Name] stock due to the following reasons:

- [Reason 1: e.g., Pending Earnings Release]

- [Reason 2: e.g., Pending Acquisition Announcement]

- [Additional Reasons]

**4. Scope**

This blackout period applies to all employees, directors, and officers
of [Company Name]. It is crucial to adhere to this policy to ensure
compliance with SEC regulations and maintain the integrity of our
financial markets.

**5. Restrictions**

During the blackout period:

- Employees may not buy or sell shares of [Company Name] stock.

- Employees may not share non-public information regarding [Company
Name] with others, including family members and friends.

**6. Exceptions**

The following transactions are exempt from the blackout period:

- Pre-scheduled trading plans under Rule 10b5-1.

- Transactions approved in advance by the Compliance Officer: [Name
and Contact Information].

**7. Compliance**

Failure to comply with this blackout period may result in disciplinary
action, including potential termination of employment.

**8. Questions**

If you have any questions regarding this blackout period, please contact
[Name of Compliance Officer] at [Contact Information].
