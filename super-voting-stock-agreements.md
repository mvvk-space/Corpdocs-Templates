**WHEREAS**, the Company has authorized the issuance of a class of

### 1. **Definitions**

1.1. **Super Voting Stock**: A class of stock that grants the holder
[NUMBER] votes per share, as opposed to the standard voting stock
which grants one vote per share.

1.2. **Common Stock**: The regular voting stock of the Company, with
one vote per share.

### 2. **Issuance of Super Voting Stock**

2.1. The Company agrees to issue to the Stockholder, and the Stockholder
agrees to purchase, [NUMBER] shares of Super Voting Stock for a total
purchase price of [AMOUNT] dollars.

2.2. The Super Voting Stock shall have the following rights:

- [Specify any additional rights, privileges, or limitations].

### 3. **Voting Rights**

3.1. Each share of Super Voting Stock shall entitle the holder to
[NUMBER] votes on all matters submitted to a vote of the shareholders
of the Company.

3.2. The Super Voting Stock shall remain in effect unless:

- The Company issues additional shares of stock that dilute the voting
power.

- A change in the control of the Company occurs.

### 4. **Transfer Restrictions**

4.1. The Stockholder may not transfer the Super Voting Stock without the
prior written consent of the Company.

4.2. Any transfer of Super Voting Stock shall be subject to the rights
of first refusal of the Company.

### 5. **Termination**

5.1. This Agreement shall terminate under the following conditions:

- Upon mutual written consent of the parties.

- Upon the sale or merger of the Company.

construed in accordance with the laws of the State of [STATE].

6.2. **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire
agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof and
supersedes all prior agreements or understandings.

6.3. **Amendments**: This Agreement may be amended only by a written
instrument executed by both parties.
