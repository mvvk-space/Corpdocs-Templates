### Golden Handcuffs Agreement

**THIS GOLDEN HANDCUFFS AGREEMENT** ("Agreement") is made and
entered into as of [DATE], by and between:

**[COMPANY NAME]**

a [STATE OF INCORPORATION] corporation

("Company")

AND

**[EMPLOYEE NAME]**

an individual residing at [ADDRESS]

("Employee")

**WHEREAS**, the Company desires to retain the Employee's services
and provide incentives that encourage the Employee to remain with the
Company.

### 1. **Definitions**

1.1. **Employment Period**: The period during which the Employee is
employed by the Company, commencing on [START DATE] and ending on
[END DATE] or termination of employment.

1.2. **Retention Bonus**: The bonus to be paid to the Employee as
specified in Section 2.

### 2. **Retention Bonus**

2.1. The Company agrees to pay the Employee a retention bonus of
[AMOUNT] (the "Retention Bonus") subject to the following conditions:

- The Employee must remain continuously employed with the Company
throughout the Employment Period.

- The Retention Bonus shall be paid to the Employee on [PAYMENT
DATE], provided that the Employee meets the conditions set forth in
this Agreement.

### 3. **Vesting Schedule**

3.1. The Retention Bonus shall vest according to the following schedule:

- [SPECIFY VESTING SCHEDULE, e.g., 50% after one year, 50% after two
years].

### 4. **Termination**

4.1. If the Employee's employment with the Company is terminated for any
reason prior to the end of the Employment Period, the Employee shall
forfeit any unvested portion of the Retention Bonus.

4.2. In the event of termination due to:

- (a) Resignation without Good Reason: Employee shall not receive any
portion of the Retention Bonus.

- (b) Termination for Cause: Employee shall not receive any portion of
the Retention Bonus.

- (c) Termination Without Cause: Employee shall receive any earned
portion of the Retention Bonus.

### 5. **Miscellaneous**

5.1. **Governing Law**: This Agreement shall be governed by and
construed in accordance with the laws of the State of [STATE].

5.2. **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire
agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof and
supersedes all prior agreements or understandings.

5.3. **Amendments**: This Agreement may be amended only by a written
instrument executed by both parties.
