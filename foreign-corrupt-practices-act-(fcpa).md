### **[Company Name]** Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA)
Compliance Policy

**Effective Date**: ______________

**Last Revised**: ______________

**Applies To**: All Employees, Directors, and Third-Party
Representatives of **[Company Name]**

---

### **Purpose**

This policy is to ensure compliance with the **Foreign Corrupt
Practices Act (FCPA)**, U.S. law that prohibits bribery of foreign
officials and mandates accurate recordkeeping. The policy applies to all
officers, directors, employees, agents, or any third party acting on
behalf of **[Company Name]**.

---

### **Key Definitions**

1. **Foreign Official**: Any officer or employee of a foreign
government or any department, agency, or instrumentality thereof, or a
public international organization. This includes political parties or
candidates for political office in foreign countries.

2. **Bribery**: Offering, giving, receiving, or soliciting anything
of value with the intent to influence an act or decision of a foreign
official to gain a business advantage.

---

### **Prohibited Conduct**

**[Company Name]** and its employees, officers, agents, and
third-party representatives are strictly prohibited from offering,
promising, authorizing, or giving anything of value to a foreign
official in order to:

- Obtain or retain business;

- Direct business to any person;

- Gain an improper advantage in the conduct of business.

---

### **Facilitation Payments**

**Facilitation payments** (small payments made to expedite routine
government actions) are not permitted unless explicitly approved by
**[Insert Legal or Compliance Department Contact]** and in
accordance with local laws.

---

### **Recordkeeping and Internal Controls**

**[Company Name]** is committed to maintaining accurate records of
all transactions, including the proper authorization of expenses.
Employees must ensure:

1. Transactions are recorded completely and accurately in the company's
books and records.

2. All expenses, including gifts, entertainment, and travel, must be
approved and documented in accordance with **[Company Name]**'s
internal accounting controls.

---

### **Third-Party Agents**

All third parties acting on behalf of **[Company Name]** must
comply with the FCPA. Before engaging a third party, the company must
conduct appropriate due diligence to ensure the third party will comply
with anti-corruption laws. Contracts with third parties must include
anti-bribery clauses and provisions allowing audits.

---

### **Training and Reporting**

1. **Training**: All employees and third parties engaged by
**[Company Name]** must complete annual FCPA compliance training.

2. **Reporting Violations**: Any employee who suspects a violation
of this policy or the FCPA must report the matter immediately to
**[Insert Reporting Mechanism/Hotline]** or to **[Insert
Compliance Officer Name]**. All reports will be kept confidential to
the extent possible, and retaliation against individuals reporting in
good faith is prohibited.

---
