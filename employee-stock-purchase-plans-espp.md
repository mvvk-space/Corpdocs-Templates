# **EMPLOYEE STOCK PURCHASE PLAN (ESPP)**

This Employee Stock Purchase Plan (the "Plan") is established by
**[Insert Company Name]**, a corporation organized under the laws
of **[Insert State]** (the "Company"), to provide eligible
employees the opportunity to purchase shares of the Company's common
stock.

---

## **1. PURPOSE**

The purpose of this Plan is to encourage ownership of the Company by its
employees, provide an incentive for employees to increase productivity,
and align employees' interests with those of the shareholders.

---

## **2. ELIGIBILITY**

All employees of the Company, who have been employed for at least
**[Insert Number]** months and who work a minimum of **[Insert
Number of Hours]** hours per week, are eligible to participate in the
Plan, except where prohibited by law.

---

## **3. OFFERING PERIODS**

The Plan will operate with successive offering periods, each lasting
**[Insert Length of Offering Period, e.g., 6 months]** (the
"Offering Period"). Each eligible employee may enroll at the beginning
of any Offering Period by completing the enrollment form provided by the
Company.

---

## **4. PURCHASE PRICE**

The purchase price per share (the "Purchase Price") shall be
**[Insert Percentage]%** of the fair market value of the
Company's common stock on either the first or last trading day of the
Offering Period, whichever is lower.

---

## **5. PAYROLL DEDUCTIONS**

### **5.1 Authorization:**

An employee may authorize payroll deductions in increments of
**[Insert Percentage]** to **[Insert Percentage]%** of their
compensation, subject to a maximum of **[Insert Dollar Amount or
Percentage]%** of the employee's annual compensation, to purchase
stock under the Plan.

### **5.2 Changes in Payroll Deductions:**

An employee may increase, decrease, or suspend payroll deductions during
the Offering Period by submitting a written request to the Company's
payroll department by **[Insert Deadline Date]**.

---

## **6. PURCHASE OF SHARES**

On the last trading day of each Offering Period, the Company will apply
the accumulated payroll deductions to purchase the maximum number of
whole shares of the Company's common stock at the Purchase Price.

### **6.1 Fractional Shares:**

No fractional shares will be issued under the Plan. Any payroll
deductions that are not sufficient to purchase a whole share will be
carried over to the next Offering Period, or refunded to the employee,
at the Company's discretion.

---

## **7. DELIVERY OF SHARES**

The shares purchased under the Plan will be delivered to the employee's
stock account as soon as administratively possible following the end of
the Offering Period. The shares will be issued in the employee's name or
in the name of the employee and the employee's spouse, as specified by
the employee.

---

## **8. WITHDRAWAL FROM THE PLAN**

An employee may withdraw from the Plan at any time prior to the last
trading day of an Offering Period by submitting a written notice of
withdrawal to the Company. All accumulated payroll deductions will be
refunded to the employee without interest.

---

## **9. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT**

If an employee's employment with the Company terminates for any reason,
including death, retirement, or resignation, the employee will be
withdrawn from the Plan and any unused payroll deductions will be
refunded to the employee (or the employee's estate).

---

## **10. RIGHTS NOT TRANSFERABLE**

The employee's rights under the Plan are personal and may not be
transferred, assigned, pledged, or otherwise disposed of by the
employee, other than by will or the laws of descent and distribution.

---

## **11. ADJUSTMENTS UPON CHANGES IN CAPITALIZATION**

In the event of any stock split, reverse stock split, stock dividend,
combination, or other change in the Company's capital structure, the
number of shares of common stock reserved under the Plan, the number of
shares subject to purchase, and the Purchase Price shall be
proportionally adjusted as determined by the Board of Directors.

---

## **12. PLAN ADMINISTRATION**

The Plan shall be administered by the Company's Board of Directors (the
"Board") or a committee designated by the Board. The Board shall have
full authority to interpret and administer the Plan, including making
any rules and regulations required for its operation.

---

## **13. AMENDMENT OR TERMINATION OF THE PLAN**

The Board may amend, suspend, or terminate the Plan at any time,
provided that no such action shall adversely affect any outstanding
rights to purchase stock under the Plan without the employee's consent.
If the Plan is terminated, all payroll deductions will be returned to
the participating employees.

---

## **14. NO GUARANTEE OF EMPLOYMENT**

Nothing in this Plan shall confer any right to continue in the
employment of the Company or affect the Company's right to terminate an
employee's employment at any time.

---

## **15. MISCELLANEOUS**

### **15.1 Governing Law:**

This Plan shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
of the State of **[Insert State]**, without regard to its conflict
of laws provisions.

### **15.2 Severability:**

If any provision of this Plan is deemed invalid or unenforceable, the
remainder of the Plan shall remain in full force and effect.
