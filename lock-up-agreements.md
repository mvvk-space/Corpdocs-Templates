### Lock-Up Agreement

**THIS LOCK-UP AGREEMENT** ("Agreement") is made and entered into
as of [DATE], by and between:

**[COMPANY NAME]**

a [STATE OF INCORPORATION] corporation

("Company")

AND

**[NAME OF SHAREHOLDER/INVESTOR]**

an individual residing at [ADDRESS]

("Shareholder")

**WHEREAS**, the Company is conducting an initial public offering
("IPO") of its shares and requires certain shareholders to agree to a
lock-up period regarding their shares.

### 1. **Definitions**

1.1. **Lock-Up Period**: The period beginning on the effective date
of the IPO and ending on [LOCK-UP END DATE].

1.2. **Shares**: All shares of common stock of the Company held by
the Shareholder as of the effective date of this Agreement.

### 2. **Lock-Up Obligations**

2.1. The Shareholder agrees not to sell, transfer, assign, pledge, or
otherwise dispose of any Shares during the Lock-Up Period without the
prior written consent of the Company.

2.2. The Shareholder acknowledges that the Company may impose
stop-transfer instructions with respect to the Shares during the Lock-Up
Period.

### 3. **Exceptions**

3.1. The Shareholder may transfer Shares:

- By will or intestate succession.

- To family members or to a trust for the benefit of the Shareholder or
family members, provided that the transferee agrees to be bound by this
Agreement.

### 4. **Termination**

4.1. This Agreement shall terminate upon the expiration of the Lock-Up
Period.

4.2. The Company may terminate this Agreement earlier upon written
notice to the Shareholder.

### 5. **Miscellaneous**

5.1. **Governing Law**: This Agreement shall be governed by and
construed in accordance with the laws of the State of [STATE].

5.2. **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire
agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof and
supersedes all prior agreements or understandings.

5.3. **Amendments**: This Agreement may be amended only by a written
instrument executed by both parties.
