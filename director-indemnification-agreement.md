### 1. **Indemnification**

The Company agrees to indemnify and hold harmless **[Insert Name of
Director]** (the "Indemnitee") from and against any and all losses,
expenses, claims, damages, liabilities, and judgments (including
reasonable attorneys' fees and costs) incurred by the Indemnitee in
connection with the performance of their duties as a director of the
Company.

### 2. **Scope of Indemnification**

Indemnification shall apply to any acts or omissions occurring while the
Indemnitee is serving in the capacity of a director, provided that the
Indemnitee:

- Acted in good faith and in a manner they reasonably believed to be in
the best interests of the Company.

- Did not engage in willful misconduct or gross negligence.

### 3. **Limitations**

This indemnification agreement shall not apply to matters arising from:

- Any breach of fiduciary duty owed to the Company or its shareholders.

- Any illegal or unlawful acts committed by the Indemnitee.

### 4. **Advancement of Expenses**

The Company shall advance expenses incurred by the Indemnitee in
defending any claim, action, or proceeding before the final disposition
of such matter, provided that the Indemnitee agrees to repay any such
amounts if it is ultimately determined that they are not entitled to
indemnification.

### 5. **Notification**

The Indemnitee shall promptly notify the Company of any claim, demand,
or action for which indemnification is sought, and the Company shall
have the right to assume the defense of such claim.

### 6. **Severability**

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and
effect.

### 7. **Governing Law**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the
laws of the State of **[Insert State]**.
