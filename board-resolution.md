A **board resolution** is a formal document that records decisions
made by a corporation's board of directors. It serves as an official
record of the board's actions and is typically required for significant
corporate decisions. Here's a breakdown of the key elements usually
included in a board resolution:

### Information Typically Included in a Board Resolution:

1. **Title of the Document:**

- Example: **Board Resolution of [Insert Company Name]**

2. **Date of the Meeting:**

- Example: **Date: [Insert Date]**

3. **Location of the Meeting:**

- Example: **Location: [Insert Location]**

4. **Attendees:**

- List of directors present.

- Example: **Present: [Insert Names of Directors]**

5. **Call to Order:**

- A statement indicating that the meeting was called to order.

- Example: **The meeting was called to order at [Insert Time].**

6. **Resolution Text:**

- The specific action or decision being resolved.

- Example: **Resolved, that [Insert specific action, e.g., "the
company shall enter into a contract with [Insert Company Name] for
[Insert Purpose]."]**

7. **Rationale or Background (Optional):**

- Brief explanation of why the resolution is necessary.

- Example: **Whereas, the board believes that [Insert Reason].**

8. **Voting Outcome:**

- Indicate whether the resolution passed or failed, including the vote
count if applicable.

- Example: **Voted: [Insert Number in Favor] in favor, [Insert
Number Opposed] opposed.**

9. **Signature Section:**

- Spaces for the signatures of the board members or the corporate
secretary.

- Example: **Approved by: [Insert Names and Titles]**

10. **Certification (Optional):**

- A statement certifying that the resolution is a true and correct copy
of the original.

- Example: **I, [Insert Name], Secretary of [Insert Company
Name], hereby certify that the above is a true and correct copy of the
resolution adopted by the board.**

### Example Board Resolution Layout:

---

**BOARD RESOLUTION OF [Insert Company Name]**

**Date:** [Insert Date]

**Location:** [Insert Location]

**Present:**

- [Insert Name of Director 1]

- [Insert Name of Director 2]

- [Insert Name of Director 3]

- [Insert Additional Names]

---

**Call to Order:**

The meeting was called to order at [Insert Time].

---

**RESOLUTION:**

**Resolved, that** [Insert specific action].

**Whereas,** [Insert rationale or background if applicable].

---

**Voting Outcome:**

The resolution was voted on and [passed/failed] with [Insert Number]
in favor and [Insert Number] opposed.
