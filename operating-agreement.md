### ARTICLE IV: PROFITS, LOSSES, AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**4.1 Allocation of Profits and Losses:**

Profits and losses of the Company shall be allocated to the members
based on their percentage ownership interests as shown in Article III,
unless otherwise agreed.

**4.2 Distributions:**

Distributions of the Company's assets or profits shall be made to the
members in proportion to their percentage ownership interests unless
otherwise agreed.

---

### ARTICLE V: MANAGEMENT OF THE COMPANY

**5.1 Management Structure:**

The Company shall be managed by **[Insert "the members" or "a
Manager" (if applicable)]**.

- **If managed by members**: All decisions shall require the
approval of a majority in percentage ownership interests.

- **If managed by a manager**: **[Insert Manager's Name]** is
designated as the manager of the Company and shall have full authority
to manage the affairs of the Company, except as limited by this
Agreement.

**5.2 Meetings:**

Meetings of the members may be held at such times and places as agreed
upon by the members. Regular meetings are not required, but a special
meeting may be called by any member.

**5.3 Voting Rights:**

Each member shall have voting power in proportion to their percentage
ownership interests in the Company. Decisions requiring member approval
shall be made by a majority vote unless otherwise specified.

---

### ARTICLE VI: DISSOLUTION AND TERMINATION

**6.1 Dissolution:**

The Company shall be dissolved upon the occurrence of any of the
following events:

- The unanimous written consent of all members.

- The death, bankruptcy, or withdrawal of a member, unless the
remaining members vote to continue the business.

- Any event which makes the continued operation of the Company
unlawful.

**6.2 Winding Up:**

Upon dissolution, the Company shall cease its operations and wind up its
affairs. The assets of the Company shall be liquidated, and the proceeds
distributed as follows:

1. First, to pay all liabilities and obligations of the Company.

2. Second, to the members in accordance with their ownership interests.

---

### ARTICLE VII: AMENDMENTS

This Operating Agreement may be amended or modified only by a written
agreement executed by all members of the Company.

---

### ARTICLE VIII: INDEMNIFICATION

The Company shall indemnify its members, managers, and agents against
any and all claims, losses, and liabilities arising from their acts on
behalf of the Company, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

---

laws of the state of **[Insert State]**.

**9.2 Entire Agreement:**

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding and agreement
between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements or
understandings.

**9.3 Severability:**

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remainder of the Agreement shall not be affected and
shall remain in full force and effect.

### Key Sections of the Operating Agreement:

- **Formation**: Defines the creation of the LLC and basic company
information.

- **Members and Contributions**: Specifies who the members are and
their ownership stakes.

- **Management**: Establishes whether the LLC is member-managed or
manager-managed.

- **Profits and Distributions**: Explains how profits, losses, and
distributions are shared.

- **Dissolution**: Sets out the conditions under which the LLC will
dissolve and how assets will be distributed.
