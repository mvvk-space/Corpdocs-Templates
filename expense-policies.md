# **EXPENSE POLICY**

**Company Name:** [Insert Company Name]

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

## **1. PURPOSE**

This Expense Policy outlines the guidelines for employees regarding the
reimbursement of business-related expenses incurred while performing
duties on behalf of **[Insert Company Name]**.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all employees of **[Insert Company Name]**
who incur expenses while conducting company business.

## **3. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES**

Eligible expenses include, but are not limited to:

- **Travel Expenses:**

Airfare, train tickets, mileage, car rentals, and parking.

- **Meals:**

Meals with clients or while traveling for business purposes.

- **Lodging:**

Hotel accommodations while traveling for business.

- **Supplies:**

Office supplies required for business operations.

## **4. EXPENSE LIMITS**

- **Travel Expenses:**

Maximum reimbursement of [Insert Amount] for airfare; [Insert
Amount] per mile for personal vehicle use.

- **Meals:**

Maximum reimbursement of [Insert Amount] per meal; [Insert Amount]
per day.

- **Lodging:**

Maximum reimbursement of [Insert Amount] per night.

## **5. REIMBURSEMENT PROCESS**

1. **Submission:**

Employees must submit expense reports within [Insert Time Frame] of
incurring the expense.

2. **Documentation:**

All expenses must be supported by original receipts and a brief
description of the business purpose.

3. **Approval:**

Expense reports must be approved by [Insert Title, e.g.,
Manager/Supervisor].

## **6. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES**

The following expenses will not be reimbursed:

- Personal expenses

- Alcohol (unless part of a business meal)

- Entertainment expenses unrelated to business

## **7. POLICY VIOLATIONS**

Any violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action,
including denial of reimbursement or further consequences.

## **8. POLICY REVIEW**

This policy will be reviewed annually or as needed to ensure compliance
with company standards and regulations.
