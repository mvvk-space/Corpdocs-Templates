## **1. INTRODUCTION**

This Golden Parachute Agreement ("Agreement") is made between
**[Insert Company Name]** and **[Insert Executive Name]** to
outline the terms and conditions under which the Executive will receive
severance compensation in the event of a Change in Control, as defined
herein.

## **2. DEFINITIONS**

### **2.1 Change in Control**

A "Change in Control" shall mean any of the following events:

- The acquisition of more than 50% of the voting power of the company
by any individual, entity, or group.

- A merger, consolidation, or reorganization of the company that
results in a change of the majority of the board of directors.

- A sale of all or substantially all of the company's assets.

### **2.2 Termination**

"Termination" refers to the Executive's involuntary separation from
employment without Cause or resignation for Good Reason.

## **3. SEVERANCE BENEFITS**

Upon the occurrence of a Change in Control followed by Termination, the
Executive shall receive the following severance benefits:

### **3.1 Cash Severance Payment**

- **Amount:** [Insert Amount, e.g., two times the Executive's
annual base salary]

- **Payment Timing:** [Insert Timing, e.g., within 30 days after
Termination]

### **3.2 Bonus Compensation**

- **Amount:** [Insert Amount or percentage of annual bonus]

- **Payment Timing:** [Insert Timing, e.g., prorated for the year
of Termination]

### **3.3 Stock Options and Equity Awards**

- All unvested stock options and equity awards shall immediately vest
upon Termination.

- The Executive may exercise stock options for a period of [Insert
Duration, e.g., one year] post-Termination.

### **3.4 Benefits Continuation**

- The Executive will be entitled to continuation of health benefits for
a period of [Insert Duration, e.g., 12 months] following Termination,
or until the Executive secures other health coverage.

## **4. CONDITIONS FOR RECEIVING BENEFITS**

The Executive must comply with the following conditions to receive
severance benefits:

- Signing a release of claims against the company.

- Adherence to any non-compete and non-solicitation agreements in
place.

- Compliance with confidentiality obligations.

## **5. TAX WITHHOLDING**

All payments made under this Agreement shall be subject to applicable
tax withholding as required by law.

### **6.2 Amendment**

This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.

### **6.3 Severability**

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and
effect.
