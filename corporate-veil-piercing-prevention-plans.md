### Corporate Veil Piercing Prevention Plan

**CORPORATE VEIL PIERCING PREVENTION PLAN OF [COMPANY NAME]**

**Effective Date:** [Date]

**1. Purpose**

The purpose of this Corporate Veil Piercing Prevention Plan (the "Plan")
is to ensure that [Company Name] (the "Company") operates as a
distinct legal entity, thereby reducing the risk of corporate veil
piercing and protecting the assets of the shareholders and directors.

**2. Definitions**

- **Corporate Veil Piercing:** The legal concept whereby a court
disregards the corporate entity to hold shareholders or directors
personally liable for the debts and obligations of the corporation.

- **Company:** [Company Name], a corporation organized under the
laws of the State of [State].

**3. Maintaining Separate Identity**

To maintain the separate identity of the Company, the following
practices will be implemented:

- **Separate Bank Accounts:** The Company will maintain its own
bank accounts and will not commingle personal and corporate funds.

- **Proper Documentation:** All corporate transactions will be
documented, and records will be maintained to substantiate the Company's
activities.

- **Corporate Meetings:** The Company will hold regular meetings of
the Board of Directors and maintain minutes of these meetings.

**4. Adequate Capitalization**

The Company will ensure that it is adequately capitalized to meet its
business obligations and liabilities. This includes:

- Assessing the initial funding required to operate the business
effectively.

- Regularly reviewing the Company's financial health and capital needs.

**5. Formalities and Compliance**

The Company will adhere to all legal formalities and compliance
requirements, including:

- Filing annual reports and paying necessary fees to maintain corporate
status.

- Complying with local, state, and federal laws applicable to the
Company's business operations.

**6. Clear Separation of Assets**

To reinforce the separation between personal and corporate assets, the
Company will:

- Avoid using corporate assets for personal purposes.

- Ensure all property owned by the Company is titled in the Company's
name.

**7. Insurance and Liability Coverage**

The Company will maintain adequate insurance coverage to protect against
potential liabilities, including:

- General liability insurance.

- Professional liability insurance, if applicable.

**8. Review and Update**

This Plan will be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to ensure
compliance with evolving legal standards and business practices.

**9. Governing Law**

This Plan shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
of the State of [State].
