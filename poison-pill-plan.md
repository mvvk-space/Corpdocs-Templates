### Poison Pill Plan

**POISON PILL PLAN OF [COMPANY NAME]**

**Effective Date:** [Date]

**1. Purpose**

The purpose of this Poison Pill Plan (the "Plan") is to protect the
interests of [Company Name] (the "Company") and its shareholders by
reducing the likelihood of an unsolicited takeover attempt that is
deemed not to be in the best interests of the Company and its
shareholders.

**2. Definitions**

- **Acquiring Person:** Any individual or entity that acquires, or
attempts to acquire, beneficial ownership of 15% or more of the
Company's outstanding shares.

- **Shares:** Common shares of the Company, par value $[Par
Value] per share.

**3. Triggering Events**

This Plan will be triggered if an Acquiring Person obtains beneficial
ownership of 15% or more of the Shares without the approval of the Board
of Directors.

**4. Rights Distribution**

Upon the occurrence of a Triggering Event, each holder of Shares will
receive a right (a "Right") to purchase additional Shares at a price
(the "Exercise Price") of $[Exercise Price] per Share.

**5. Adjustments**

The Exercise Price and the number of Rights will be adjusted in
accordance with the terms of this Plan to account for stock splits,
dividends, and other relevant changes in the Company's capital
structure.

**6. Exemptions**

The following transactions are exempt from this Plan:

- Acquisitions approved by the Board of Directors.

- Acquisitions by employee benefit plans of the Company.

**7. Administration**

This Plan will be administered by a committee appointed by the Board of
Directors, which has the authority to interpret the Plan and make
determinations regarding the implementation and enforcement of the Plan.

**8. Amendments**

This Plan may be amended or terminated by the Board of Directors at any
time, provided that no such amendment or termination shall affect the
Rights then outstanding.

**9. Governing Law**

This Plan shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
of the State of [State].
