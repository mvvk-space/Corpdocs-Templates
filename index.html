Corpdocs Templates

Templates and reference write-ups for corporate documents — from incorporation and bylaws through IPO prospectuses and crisis-management plans.

Repository: mvvk-space/Corpdocs-Templates

What's here

70+ one-page write-ups, one per document type. Each explains what the document is, what it covers, and when you need it:

  • Formation — articles & certificate of incorporation, bylaws, operating agreements
  • Board & governance — meeting minutes, resolutions, quorum certificates, shadow board notes, succession planning
  • Shareholders & equity — shareholder agreements, capitalization tables, stock option agreements, ESPPs, golden handcuffs/parachutes, super-voting stock
  • Compliance & policy — SOX documentation, FCPA, insider trading policies, whistleblower policy, data privacy
  • Transactions — M&A documents, joint ventures, private placement memoranda, lock-up agreements, IPO prospectuses, roadshow materials
  • Employment — employment contracts, non-competes, NDAs, executive compensation
  • Crisis & comms — crisis management plan, dark-site plan, public apology statements, proxy fight playbook, poison pill plan

See 1corpdoc-index.md for the full index, and file-naming-conventions.md for naming rules (lowercase, dashes, .md, dated items as YYYY-MM-DD-...).
